The White Cat

Pure, graceful, and endlessly curious

🐱

Snow-White and Serene

White cats are among the most striking and elegant of all domestic cats. With their pristine coats and often vivid blue or odd-colored eyes, they carry an air of quiet mystery. Each one is unique — some are playful and mischievous, others calm and contemplative, but all are undeniably beautiful.

👁️

Striking Eyes

White cats often have blue, green, gold, or odd-colored eyes (one of each color), which is called heterochromia.

🧬

Rare Coat

True white cats make up only about 5% of the domestic cat population. Their color comes from a special masking gene.

🔇

Possible Deafness

White cats with blue eyes have a higher chance of being deaf in one or both ears due to a genetic link affecting the inner ear.

☀️

Sun Sensitive

Their pale skin underneath means white cats can be susceptible to sunburn and should have limited direct sun exposure.
